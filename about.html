Corporate Identity

PIT2PORT GLOBAL RESOURCES (PTY) LTD

PIT2PORT GLOBAL RESOURCES (PTY) LTD is a South African logistics specialist focused on mine-to-market solutions for bulk and specialist commodity sales globally. We support clients operating in demanding and time-sensitive environments by providing logistics services that are practical, efficient, and commercially aligned.

Our role is to ensure that commodities move safely and effectively from source to destination through coordinated inland transport, cross-border handling, port services, and vessel loading. We understand that in commodity markets, logistics is more than transportation.
