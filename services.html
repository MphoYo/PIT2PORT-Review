Core Capabilities

Our Services

PIT2PORT GLOBAL RESOURCES (PTY) LTD provides integrated logistics solutions for bulk and specialist commodities moving from production source to export or end-user destination. Our services are designed to reduce complexity, improve flow reliability, and support commercially sound movement of commodities through the supply chain.

01

Inland Transportation

We coordinate inland transport from mine sites and inland collection points to border posts, storage facilities, and ports. Our focus is on dependable planning, route efficiency, cargo integrity, and consistent movement.

Key Commercial Benefits:

  • Optimized truck-turnaround limits wait delays.
  • Secured rail capacity decreases ton-mile rates.
  • Integrated GPS geo-fencing provides transit security.
Request This Service
02

Cross-Border Handling

Cross-border logistics can be one of the most sensitive points in any regional supply chain. We help manage border processes with the aim of reducing delays, improving visibility, and maintaining flow continuity across jurisdictions.

Key Commercial Benefits:

  • Fast-tracked clearances eliminate border queue charges.
  • Vetted compliance reduces tax penalty exposure.
  • Secure layover yards maintain cargo security.
Request This Service
03

Port Storage

We arrange port-side storage solutions that support continuity between inland delivery and vessel loading. Effective storage planning helps reduce bottlenecks and improves cargo readiness for export.

Key Commercial Benefits:

  • Physical segregation prevents mineral cross-contamination.
  • High storage capacity buffers inland delivery variance.
  • Grade blending ensures shipment complies with assays.
Request This Service
04

Port Handling

We manage port handling activities to ensure that commodities are moved through port infrastructure in a controlled, efficient, and professionally coordinated manner.

Key Commercial Benefits:

  • Optimized reclaim routes limit conveyor stoppages.
  • High-intensity terminal handling ensures rapid load cycles.
  • Preventative maintenance structures reduce belt failures.
Request This Service
05

Vessel Loading

We support vessel-loading operations with a strong focus on timing, coordination, and cargo handling efficiency to help ensure smooth transfer from port facility to marine transport.

Key Commercial Benefits:

  • Vessel laycan coordination avoids pre-loading delay fees.
  • Rapid ship loading reduces vessel port stays.
  • Efficient draft sequencing prevents vessel load errors.
Request This Service
06

Supply Chain Solution Development

Beyond execution, we design supply chain models that solve practical trade challenges. Where conventional logistics routes may not support viable trade, we work to structure solutions that make movement possible and commercially sustainable.

Key Commercial Benefits:

  • Rigorous simulation removes trial-and-error risks.
  • Accurate capital sizing prevents over-spending.
  • Resiliency audits protect against single-point failures.
